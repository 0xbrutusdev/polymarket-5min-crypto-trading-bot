# Simulation-loss root-cause audit, October 2026

Scope: why the simulation mode of the Polymarket BTC 5-minute maker bot lost
money, whether the loss says anything about the strategy, and which changes
survive an out-of-sample test under conservative fill assumptions. Live mode
has never traded (the funder wallet holds 0 USDC); every loss on record is
simulated.

All replay numbers below are on the frozen dataset: 384 windows with cached
public taker prints and Binance 1-s spot, 2026-10-11 18:20 to 2026-10-13
07:50 UTC, split by time into 230 training windows (before 2026-10-12 19:05)
and 154 test windows. The list is in `data/backtest/audit-dataset.json`.
PnL is USDC per 5-minute window before the maker rebate unless stated.

## 2. What the simulation now is

`spork30/simulate.py` steps the real engine once per second:

- spot, price-to-beat and realised sigma from the Binance `SpotFeed` (seeded with 1-s klines so the beat and vol exist from the first tick);
- a top of book rebuilt from Polymarket public taker prints polled every 2 s for the live window's `condition_id`;
- a resting ask fills only when a taker BUY print on that outcome trades through its price, after `SIM_QUEUE_AHEAD` shares at the same level, through `spork30/fillmodel.py`, the module the replay uses. An ask placed after a print was already on the tape cannot meet it.

The health pane states what is measured (prints, prices, sizes, timing) and what is modelled (queue position, print-implied touch, the assumption our asks change nobody's behaviour). Refactoring the replay onto `fillmodel` left its results bit-identical (48-window fingerprint `20833825200925a2` before and after).

## 3. Accounting changes

- `cycle_closed` now carries `proceeds`, `sold_up`, `sold_down`, `merged`, `redeemed`, `split_size`, `winner`; a gap in the identity is logged as `pnl_mismatch`, not raised.
- The `redeem` record's `size` is what the books moved; a winner residual below `dust_shares` is reported as `dust` instead. This also stops `_undo_redeem` restoring cash that never moved.
- `redeem` is logged before `cycle_closed`, so a reader replaying the log has every leg when it checks the booked cash. The screen's `Book` keeps an independent tally and shows `pnl mismatch N` when it cannot reproduce the engine's figure (0 on the 633-cycle log).
- "adverse: sold winner %" on the PnL pane, from `cycle_closed` winners.

### Hypotheses and verdicts

Protocol (`spork30/audit.py`): each hypothesis is a knob that is off at 0. A small grid is replayed on the 230 training windows under both conservative fill models (uniform queue 250; touch-only queue 100), spot lag 2 s, rebate 0; the grid point with the best average train mean is the one candidate judged on the 154 test windows. Acceptance requires, under **both** fill models: test mean above baseline, worst window not worse, the block-bootstrap 95% interval of the paired per-window improvement (candidate − baseline on identical windows, hourly blocks) excluding zero, and a non-negative improvement in every volatility tercile. The rule was fixed before the test numbers were seen.

Two deviations from the plan as written, both decided before the test set was touched:

- H1 was planned as a symmetric guard, `|fair − touch| > k·sigma`. The diagnostics showed the loss sits only on the side where fair is *at or above* the touch, while fair-below-touch fills were the one profitable bucket; a symmetric guard would discard them. The knob implemented is directional and in ticks: withhold an ask that would rest `FAIR_GAP_WITHHOLD_TICKS` or more above its touch.
- The CI criterion is on the paired improvement rather than on the candidate's own mean, which is what "CI excludes zero improvement" has to mean on identical windows.

Across the four hypotheses, the two that reduce shares sold on the adversely-selected side (H1, and H4's cooldown) are the two that improve the conservative uniform-queue model; neither change helps when fills are easier (touch-only). That is consistent with cause 3: the loss comes from being swept, and the remedy is quoting less where sweeps reach us, at the cost of volume. How much volume is worth giving up depends on the true queue position, which is unresolved (§5).

### What is shipped

Defaults are unchanged: all four knobs are 0. H1 is the strongest result in this audit and the only candidate whose improvement interval excludes zero under a conservative fill model, but it missed a pre-registered criterion by 1.5 USDC on one window of 154. Shipping it on that evidence would be tuning the rule to the result. The honest next step is more data: with the print-driven simulation now recording real fills, the same `audit experiments` run on a longer, later window set decides it.

## 5. What the data cannot show

- **True queue position.** Public prints show what traded, not who was ahead. The uniform-250 and touch-only-100 models bracket plausible positions; the sign of the baseline changes between queue 100 and 250 at lag 1. There is no public measurement that settles this; only live fills will.
- **Live latency.** The replay assumes the spot print of 1–3 s ago; the live loop refuses spot older than 1.5 s. The actual order round-trip and fill reporting latency on the CLOB are unmeasured because live has never traded. Expectancy loses about 1 USDC per window per second of lag.
- **Fees and rebate.** The 42 bps maker rebate is an estimate from the reference account's payments and is never booked; it is shown separately. Taker fees are zero on these markets today. Either can change.
- **Sample.** Two days, 384 windows, 32 hourly blocks, one BTC regime (Oct 11–13). The test set is friendlier than the training set at every setting. The `fair_edge = 0.03` default was itself chosen on 96 of these windows, so the training set is partly in-sample for the baseline.
- **Book reconstruction.** The touch is inferred from the last print on each side, not observed; between prints it can be stale. Our own asks are assumed not to change anybody else's behaviour.
- **Settlement.** The replay's winner is the market's resolution; the simulation reads the Chainlink TWAP against the beat ~65 s after the window. These should agree but have not been cross-checked at scale.
